Her Journey

A story that deserves to be remembered.

Miracle's story is one of survival, resilience and the power of love. After a life-changing tragedy, her family faced a long road through hospitalization, rehabilitation, caregiving and healing.

This website is a place to celebrate Miracle's life, preserve her memories and share the message of hope behind her journey.

Her name is Miracle.”
