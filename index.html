Her Journey

A story that deserves to be remembered.

Miracle's story is one of survival, resilience and unconditional love. After a life-changing tragedy, her family faced hospitalization, rehabilitation, caregiving, uncertainty and countless moments of prayer.

Through every difficult moment, Miracle continued to show strength. Her journey became a reminder that even in the darkest moments, hope can remain.

“Her life is a testimony.
Her name is Miracle.”

Miracle Survivor

Written by Destiny Robinson

Miracle Survivor shares Miracle's journey through tragedy, rehabilitation, caregiving, faith and healing. It is a story about a mother's love, a child's strength and the hope that carried a family forward.

The Author

About Destiny Robinson

Destiny Robinson is Miracle's mother and the author of Miracle Survivor. She shares their journey to preserve Miracle's story, honor every step of her survival and give hope to families facing their own difficult seasons.

This website was created to celebrate Miracle, document her journey and give everyone who has supported her family a place to follow her story.
